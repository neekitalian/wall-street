# Glossary

**Adverse selection** — Risk that a counterparty trades because it has superior information (Ch 1).

**Basis risk** — Risk that an exposure and its hedge stop moving together (Ch 8).

**BATNA** — Best alternative to a negotiated agreement; the no-deal benchmark (Ch 13).

**Bid-ask spread** — Difference between dealer purchase and sale prices, compensating immediacy and risk (Ch 1).

**Carry** — Income or cost from holding a position through time (Ch 7, 9, 10).

**Catalyst** — Event or process expected to realize a thesis (Ch 7, 9, 10).

**CDO** — Vehicle repackaging credit exposures into claims with different priorities (Ch 9).

**Collateral haircut** — Discount applied to collateral value in determining financing (Ch 8).

**Counterparty risk** — Risk that the other contracting party cannot perform (Ch 9, 10).

**Credit default swap (CDS)** — Contract exchanging premium for protection against defined credit events or losses (Ch 9).

**Cramdown** — Bankruptcy-plan confirmation over dissent when legal requirements are met (Ch 13).

**Duration** — Approximate bond-price sensitivity to interest-rate changes (Ch 2).

**Enterprise value** — Value of operating assets available to all capital providers (Ch 3, 12).

**Exit multiple** — Valuation multiple assumed when an investment is sold or listed (Ch 3).

**Fulcrum security** — Claim located near the valuation boundary and likely to receive reorganized equity (Ch 12).

**Global macro** — Strategy trading currencies, rates, commodities, and related assets based on regimes and policy (Ch 6, 7).

**Gross exposure** — Sum of long exposure and absolute short exposure (Ch 7).

**Guarantee** — Promise by one entity to satisfy another entity's obligation (Ch 12).

**Leverage** — Financing or contractual structure that magnifies changes in equity or capital (Ch 3, 8).

**Liquidity horizon** — Time needed to reduce a position without unacceptable market impact (Ch 10).

**Long-short equity** — Strategy combining long and short positions to isolate selection or control exposures (Ch 6, 7).

**Material nonpublic information** — Important information not broadly public, subject to fact-specific legal analysis (Ch 5).

**Merger arbitrage** — Trading transaction spreads and completion probabilities (Ch 5).

**Net exposure** — Long exposure minus short exposure (Ch 7).

**Overfitting** — Fitting noise or research choices that fail to generalize (Ch 11).

**Recovery waterfall** — Allocation of distributable value among claims by priority and legal rights (Ch 12).

**Reflexivity** — Feedback in which beliefs and market actions alter fundamentals or future behavior (Ch 7, 15).

**Relative-value arbitrage** — Trading price relationships while hedging some broad direction (Ch 6, 8).

**Restructuring support agreement** — Contract documenting stakeholder support for a restructuring subject to terms (Ch 13).

**Secured debt** — Debt supported by liens on specified collateral (Ch 12).

**Signal** — Measurable input used to forecast return or risk (Ch 11).

**Sources and uses** — Table showing acquisition funding sources and required cash uses (Ch 3).

**Structural subordination** — Parent-level claim dependence on residual value after subsidiary creditors (Ch 12).

**Value at Risk (VaR)** — Model-based loss estimate for a stated horizon and confidence level (Ch 8).
